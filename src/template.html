Classics

Jasmine

Scented with the aroma of jasmine flowers, jasmine tea aids in calming the mind, boosts the immune system, and improves mental health. It is one of the founder's favorite tea.

Ginseng

Made from the Ginseng plant roots. The Ginseng is a delectable brew that reduces fatigue, the Earth Kingdom elites like to infuse this with honey to counter its bitter taste.

White Dragon

A tea so delicious that it is heartbreaking! It is brewed from the leaves and flowers of the rare White Dragon Bush. Most locals mistake this with the White Jade bush which is lethal.
